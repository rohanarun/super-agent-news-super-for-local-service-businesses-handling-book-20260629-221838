Why bookings and quotes are becoming an agent problem

Google’s agents now call you

Gemini 3.5 Flash can operate computers and is already being used to call home repair, beauty, and pet care businesses to collect pricing and availability — often without a human ever visiting your site.

Speed and structure win

Agentic booking pilots show agents will hang up if they can’t get a structured quote in under ~90 seconds. Delayed callbacks don’t convert.

Replies happen everywhere

Bookings and questions now arrive via phone, email, web forms, Google Business Profile, and marketplaces. Humans copy-pasting between tools doesn’t scale.

Computer use is the new baseline

Google has made computer use a first-class capability inside Gemini 3.5 Flash, confirming that real desktop control — not just chat — is where agents are headed.
